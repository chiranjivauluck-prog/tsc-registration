Works on phones and computers; nothing to install. The form runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent to or stored on any server or database. Everything stays on your own device until you print or save it. Use Start over at any time to clear it.

Please use your own details or made-up details. Do not enter another person’s real name or phone number.

What UAT is testing

The sangat fills in the form online, prints it or saves it as a PDF, and brings it to Satsang as today. The printed sheet should arrive complete, legible and already added up, so it can go straight to the Finance desk with no checking. UAT confirms three things:

  1. The form guides a first-time user through every scenario without confusion.
  2. The printed sheet gives Accounting everything needed to take payment and issue wristbands, tokens and passes.
  3. The printed sheet gives Data Entry everything needed to enter the registration exactly as a paper form would.

How a UAT round works

1 · Fill inPick a scenario below and complete the form on your phone or computer, exactly as a sangat member would.
2 · Print / Save as PDFOn the last step press Print / Save as PDF and choose Save as PDF. Name the file UAT-S03-YourName.pdf (scenario number, your name).
3 · SendEmail the PDF to both Accounting and Data Entry (addresses at the bottom). One email per scenario is easiest.
4 · Teams processAccounting fulfils the form virtually (totals, payment, what would be issued). Data Entry enters it into the registration records as a real form. Both log anything missing or unclear.

Every PDF is a test. Nothing is registered, no payment is taken, and the records Data Entry creates are test records to be removed after the round.

Test scenarios

Each scenario says what to enter and what you should see. If what you see is different, that is a finding: note it in the issue log below. Testers who have time are welcome to invent their own scenario as number 11.

1Family by Study Centre bus
2 adults and 1 child aged 8, both days, Study Centre Shuttle Buses, Option C Park and Ride from Mohawk College, 5:30 am both days, 3 tickets each day, 1 car, 3 teas and 3 lunches each day, paying by cheque.
Expect: a one-page sheet; bus total $60 each day; tea and lunch $21 each day; total payment $162; 1 guardian for 1 child accepted.
2Special needs and Request to Park
2 adults, both days. Answer Yes to special needs for 2 members, then choose Request to Park at the Study Centre, 2 spots, and tick the acknowledgement. Fill in both Special Needs forms (one with a companion, one with a wheelchair).
Expect: the printout has 3 pages (sheet plus 2 supplements); the parking line reads “2 spots (2 Special Needs)”; an orange note about 2 or more spots; no bus tickets or bus total anywhere.
3Young child
2 adults and 1 child aged 4, Saturday only. Open Transportation.
Expect: Study Centre Shuttle Buses and Hotel Shuttle Buses are shown as unavailable with a reason (children 5 or under must come by car). Choose Uber / Taxi / Drop-off; the sheet shows the drop-off address. Request to Park is also available because of the child.
4Hotel shuttle, one day
3 adults, Sunday only, Hotel Shuttle Buses, Brampton: Courtyard by Marriott, 3 tickets.
Expect: only Sunday is offered for tickets; the departure time is fixed; total $30; the sheet names the hotel.
5Charter bus arranged by a Centre
4 adults, both days, Charter Buses, arranged by Local Satsang Centre, enter a Centre name and departure location, 4 teas each day, no lunch, paying cash.
Expect: no bus ticket step; the sheet shows the organizer and departure location; total $8.
6Large family, several cars
4 adults and 2 children aged 7 and 12, both days, Option A Orangeville Fairgrounds, 6 tickets each day, then set cars parking to 3.
Expect: an orange warning that most families arrive in one or two cars, but the form still continues. Cars cannot be set higher than the day’s tickets.
7Translation and hearing assistance
3 adults, Saturday only, 2 members needing Punjabi/Hindi to English translation, 1 needing hearing assistance, Option B Brampton Fairgrounds, 3 tickets.
Expect: the counts cannot exceed 3; the sheet lists translation and hearing counts separately.
8Make mistakes on purpose
Leave required fields blank, type a 9-digit phone number and a postal code like 12345, list 5 children with no guardian, ask for more bus tickets than people listed, enter special needs for 4 members in a family of 2.
Expect: each mistake is flagged next to the field as you type, in plain words; Continue and Print stay disabled until fixed; nothing is lost while you correct it.
9Draft and Start over
Fill in the first two steps, close the browser completely, then reopen the link. Then press Start over.
Expect: your draft is restored with a message; Start over clears everything and returns to step 1.
10Phone and computer
Repeat scenario 1 on a phone and again on a computer. Save the PDF from each.
Expect: both PDFs are one page, identical in content, and readable when printed.

Tester checklist for every printed sheet

  • Name, phone, city, province and postal code are complete and readable.
  • The attending table shows the right people on the right days.
  • Only the transportation option you chose appears, with its details.
  • Bus, tea and lunch totals match what you entered, and the grand total adds up.
  • Payment method shows; the signature and date lines are blank and ready to sign.
  • The footer shows the version and “build 1.4 UAT”.
  • Special Needs supplements, if any, follow as extra pages numbered with the sheet.

For the Accounting team

Treat each PDF as a form handed to you at the Finance desk. Without contacting the tester:

  1. Confirm the totals: recompute bus, tea and lunch amounts and the grand total from the quantities shown.
  2. Record what you would issue: wristbands per day, Family Area tokens, translation and hearing tokens, parking pass, bus tickets by location and time.
  3. Note anything you had to guess, ask about, or could not find on the sheet.

The aim is to decide whether a form from the online page can go straight to payment with no checking.

For the Data Entry team

Enter each PDF into the registration records exactly as you would a paper form. Then:

  1. Note any field the system needs that the sheet does not provide, or provides in a different form or order.
  2. Note anything that slowed you down compared with a well-filled paper form.
  3. Mark every record you create as a UAT test so it can be removed after the round.
