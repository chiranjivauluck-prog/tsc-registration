The online registration form: how it works and why it is safe

A plain-language summary for approval · October 2026

In one paragraph

Today the sangat downloads a PDF from torontoprogram.org, prints it, fills it in by hand and brings it to Satsang. The proposal is to add a second option on the same website: a web page that asks the same questions one at a time, checks the answers as they are typed, adds up the totals, and prints the same one-page form ready to sign and bring in. It stores nothing anywhere. The sangat’s details stay on their own phone or computer, exactly as a printed PDF does today. The paper form continues unchanged for anyone who prefers it.

What we are asking leadership to approve: adding this page to the website alongside the existing PDF, and a “fast lane” at registration for forms completed this way.

What happens to the information

1 · OpenThe page loads from the website — nothing else is downloaded or installed.
2 · Fill inEverything typed stays inside the browser on that device. The website never receives it.
3 · PrintThe browser prints the form, or saves a PDF, on that device.
4 · Hand inThe signed paper comes to Satsang as today. The only copy is the one in the sangat’s hand.

This is the same privacy position as the current PDF: the Centre receives a piece of paper, and nothing more. The difference is only that the paper arrives complete, legible and already added up.

Security and privacy — the plain facts

ConcernPosition
Is personal information collected?No. The page has no database, no server-side code and no account system. There is nothing on our side that could store, see or leak a name or phone number.
Is anything sent over the internet when someone types?No. Once the page has loaded, it works with the internet switched off. Nothing is transmitted while filling in or printing.
Could the data be hacked from the website?There is no data on the website to hack. The page is a single file of text; the only thing an attacker could obtain from the site is the blank form itself.
What about a lost or shared phone?A draft is kept on the device only so a person can finish later, and a “Start over” button wipes it. It is no different from a PDF saved to that phone — and unlike a PDF, it is cleared with one tap.
Does it use cookies, tracking or analytics?None. No third-party scripts, no advertising, no visitor tracking.
Does the Centre need to manage passwords or accounts?No. There is nothing to log in to, so nothing to reset, breach or support.
Privacy law (PIPEDA)Because no personal information is collected or stored by the organisation through the page, the page itself creates no new obligations. The same privacy and consent wording as the paper form is printed on every sheet.

What leadership gains at Satsang

A fast lane at registration

Because a form from the online page cannot be printed until it is complete and the totals are already calculated, it needs no checking. Sangat arriving with one can go straight to the Finance team or the FA/SNP desk to pay and collect wristbands. Even if only 10–20% use it in the first year, that is 10–20% fewer forms to check — and others, seeing the shorter queue, will choose it next year.

Fewer errors to chase afterwards

Illegible phone numbers, missing postal codes, bus tickets for people not listed, children without a guardian, parking requests from families who are not eligible — each of these is caught before printing, not after.

No new burden on sevadars

Nothing changes at the desk except that some forms arrive ready. There is no system to log in to, no data to look after, no passwords to reset, and no training beyond “this form is already checked”.

What it is not

It is not online registration. Nobody is registered by using the page — registration still happens when the signed paper is handed in and payment is made, exactly as today. The page is simply a better way to fill in the form.
  • It does not replace the PDF or the paper form; both remain available.
  • It does not send anything to the Centre, to the website, or to anyone.
  • It does not take payment and holds no financial details.
  • It does not require the sangat to create an account or give an email address.

Current status

A working preview exists and has been tested on phones and computers. Sections 1–10, the Special Needs supplement, Shuttle Buses, Charter Buses, Uber/Taxi and Request to Park are complete; Hotel Shuttle Buses is the one remaining option.
