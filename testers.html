The idea behind it

The sangat fills in the form online, prints it (or saves it as a PDF), and brings it to Satsang as they do today. Because every answer is checked as it is typed — required fields, phone and postal code format, the one-parent-per-four-children rule, who is eligible for parking, and so on — the printed sheet arrives already validated and with the totals calculated.

That opens the door to a fast lane: sangat arriving with an online-completed form could go straight to the Finance team or the FA/SNP desk to pay and register, with no checking of the form needed. If 10, 15 or 20% of the sangat use it in the first year, others will see them moving through quickly and want to use it too.

Once again: nothing is saved on a server or database. It is all saved locally on their phone, laptop or PC. They print it and bring it to Satsang.

What is done in this build

  • Sections 1–5: contact, who’s attending, translation / hearing / special needs
  • Section 6: Study Centre Shuttle Buses (with Section 7 bus tickets), Charter Buses, Uber / Taxi / Drop-off, Request to Park
  • Sections 9–10: Tea & packed lunch, total payment, cash / cheque
  • Review page and Print / Save as PDF — a one-page sheet to sign and hand in

Please try these on your phone

  1. Family by bus — 2 adults + 1 child aged 8, both days, Park and Ride from Mohawk College, 4 teas and 3 lunches each day, paying by cheque. Print the sheet.
  2. Special needs, parking — answer Yes to special needs, then choose Request to Park. Notice the reason is pre-filled.
  3. Young child — list a child aged 5 or under. Notice the bus option becomes unavailable and explains why; choose Uber / Taxi / Drop-off instead.
  4. Make mistakes on purpose — leave fields blank, enter a 9-digit phone number, add children with no parent. Check the messages make sense.
